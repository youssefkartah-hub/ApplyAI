Connect your Google Sheet

Your hourly routine fills a Google Sheet — paste its published CSV link here and this dashboard becomes the front-end for it.

  1. Open your tracking Google Sheet.
  2. File → Share → Publish to web.
  3. Pick the sheet tab, choose Comma-separated values (.csv), click Publish.
  4. Copy the link it gives you and paste it below.
