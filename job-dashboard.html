Connect your Notion tracker

This dashboard reads your Notion "Job Application Tracker" through a private token.

  1. Create an integration at notion.so/my-integrations → New integration → Internal → copy the secret (ntn_…).
  2. Open your Job Application Tracker in Notion → top-right ••• → Connections → add your integration.
  3. Create a file named notion_token.txt in the app folder and paste the secret into it.
  4. Restart the app (re-run python3 notion_server.py) and refresh this page.

Tip: click any day to open those applications in the Applications tab.
